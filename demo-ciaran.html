DEMO

Set up the three apps

About 5 minutes. Everything inside is made-up sample data for "Sam Demo", a part-time reseller in Leicester (fakeTesco, fakeAsda, fakePokemonCentre…). It goes back to how it started every night at 3am, so let people tap anything.

1FlipCheck

Is this deal worth buying? Profit after fees and postage, and what to offer.

Open FlipCheck

2ListMe

Listings for eBay, Vinted and Facebook, buyer replies, and what to do today.

Open ListMe

3Receipts

Snap a receipt, track sales, parcels to post, returns and profit. Already connected to the demo: nothing to paste.

Open Receipts

Signing in (FlipCheck and ListMe)

  1. Tap Open FlipCheck. Type your email and tap Send me a code.
  2. Open the email from Cloudflare, copy the 6-digit code, paste it in. You stay signed in for weeks.
  3. ListMe uses the same email and code.

Put each one on your home screen (Android, Chrome)

  1. With the app open in Chrome, tap ⋮ (three dots, top right).
  2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Install / Add.
  3. Do the same for all three. Open them from the home screen icons from then on.

If Chrome opens a different browser's page, copy the link and paste it into Chrome.
